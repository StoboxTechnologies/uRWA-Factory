Prototypes

All four surfaces from the uRWA Factory specification, clickable. Nothing connects to a chain — every value maps to a real function in the docs.

Deploy console

Six-step token creation, straight from TokenParams. Warns before irreversible choices, shows the resulting rule set before you commit, and catches an allowlist paired with a regime it cannot satisfy.

Token console

Every facet with its settings, compliance configuration, supply, holders grouped by identity, roles, and contract verification. The emergency facet is opt-in and shown as not installed.

Public verifier

Check any token's conformance, rules, provenance and attestations — then simulate a transfer and see exactly which rule would refuse it, before anyone signs. No account, no wallet.

Investor page

Position split into transferable and locked with unlock dates, your eligibility line by line, and a transfer that is checked for free before any signature is requested. Toggle the wallet chip to see the unverified state.
