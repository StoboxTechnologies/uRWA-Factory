Stobox Orbit

The whole Web3 product under one name – the open-source uRWA Factory a client can deploy, our hosted Factory, Intelligence, the signed oracle and the agents – reviewed from six institutional seats: custodian, auditor, compliance officer, enterprise buyer, infrastructure architect, asset-class specialist. Gene's frame: one factory whose tokens meet every institutional requirement by design, so that when a custodian, a bank network or a commodity issuer arrives, nothing is rebuilt – only partners and attestations are added. The first client is Stobox itself, with STBX, operated by one person today.

2 · One flow

A coupon, from the administrator's signature to the custodian's confirmation

The flow every asset class shares in some form: a corporate action is signed by the parties the channel policy names, the token snapshots entitlements by person, the payout executes under a mandate, the money moves on chain or is attested by a paying agent, the term is marked executed, and the custodian receives a standard message. Nothing in this flow exists in code yet except the DvP leg.

3b · Roles that move

Ruled 25.09.2026: the technical role must be transferable – to a second Safe, to a partner, to a TA – without a redeploy. Design:

  • Every role is held by an address, never by a person; two-step handover: the holder proposes a successor, the successor accepts, an event names both. No role can be left empty (already enforced in RolesFacet._remove).
  • The technical role is split from the issuer role: UPGRADE_ADMIN administers itself, ISSUER_ADMIN administers the business roles – closing G5 – so a second technical Safe or a partner can take one without the other.
  • A handover of UPGRADE_ADMIN runs under the upgrade delay; a handover of COMPLIANCE_OFFICER or SUPPLY_OPERATOR is immediate, because an issuer must be able to replace an operator the same day.
  • Delegation without transfer: a mandate (AgentAuthority) lets a partner act within bounds while the role stays home – the model for a custodian instructing on a client's behalf.
  • Today: Gene holds everything and operates alone; the mechanics let that change one role at a time, later.

Deferred by ruling – not on the critical path

  • The Safe topology (G19). Gene: a separate technical Safe will be set up later; ignore it now. Recorded as deferred, not as a blocker. The role-handover mechanics above are what make "later" cheap.
  • Custodian, TA, administrator and bank partners. None today; the hooks are built, the partners come with the first institutional client.
  • SOC 1 / SOC 2, insurance, escrow, SLA credits. Contract-level items that follow the first enterprise contract, not precede STBX.
  • AML programme and MLRO. Required before the first third-party issuer on the hosted Factory. For STBX, BVI counsel states in the Phase 2 package what AML duties STEL and Innovations carry and whether a programme and an MLRO are needed before Phase 3; no procedure is assumed to exist until it is written.

5 · Analysis

Where Orbit sets the category, where it follows, what it missed

Genuinely different – verified against the market

  • The record with tiered provenance. None of Securitize, Tokeny, Fireblocks, Chainlink, Kinexys or Canton carries tier_held, confidence, as_of, valid_until, document_id + locator and an evidenceHash on every fact. Securitize's MCP answers what exists on chain; Orbit answers who said it, when, on which document.
  • Multi-party signed oracle channels. ISSUER + RELAY + ATTESTOR / REGULATED with a threshold per instrument – a chain of responsibility a regulator can read, closer to what MAS GFIF asks than oracle-as-a-service.
  • Terms as code. A corporate action executes only if its ref is a term in the signed root. Neither DS Protocol nor T-REX links document to executable term.
  • Mandates by construction unable to mint, freeze, seize, cut or pause. Nobody in the set has it – and today it is a promise (G11).
  • An open-source factory the client deploys, with no Stobox role left on the token. DS and T-REX are open source too, but hosted; ours is designed for "we control the keys" buyers.
  • Deterministic settlement: canTransfer never reverts, whyBlocked names the rule, both legs in one transaction – the ICMA DvP target model.

Behind – said plainly

  • Custody: no integrated qualified custodian; Securitize lists BNY, Anchorage, Copper, Fireblocks.
  • Cross-chain: dropped by design ("one register"); defensible for a regulated buyer, but a "No" in every RFP row where BUIDL runs on nine chains.
  • Track record: STBX on Arbitrum, 107 holders, no audit, register rebuilt by hand; against Securitize's $3.4B AUM and a public listing.
  • Licensed venue: none; the Board is a BVI bulletin board with a perimeter question open.
  • Assurance: no SOC 1, no SOC 2, no external audit, no insurance.
  • RFP score: of 72 questions a regulated buyer sends, about 12 pass today, about 30 more after Phase 3, about 18 in Phases 4–6, and 12 never – 5 by canon (no full white-label, no venue, no percentage of a raise, no bps, no professional services for self-serve), 7 simply not done.

What we had missed entirely

  1. Bank money as a cash leg (JPMD on Base, EMT under MiCA, the TCH deposit-token network targeted for 2027).
  2. Collateral mobility and repo – what turned BUIDL from a fund into infrastructure.
  3. Fund-administrator and paying-agent integration as signer roles with contracts behind them.
  4. ISO 20022 for corporate actions and settlement messages.
  5. Standard-body presence: ERC3643 Association, Canton Foundation and CMTA are where buyers look; ERC-7943 and CMTAT compatibility are ours to claim.
  6. Insurance, verified escrow, SLA credits, audit rights, client sandbox, issuer SDK, tax reporting.
  7. Disaster recovery, RTO/RPO, multi-region, on-call.
  8. A written AML programme, an MLRO, daily sanctions screening, blackout policy.
  9. Schedule channels, currency and calendar on channels, suspension events, look-through.
  10. A Basel SCO60 position and a permissioned deployment option.

What must never be claimed

  • "Custody", "safekeeping", "qualified custodian" – Stobox holds no keys of clients and is no trust company.
  • "Settlement finality" beyond what Base gives – atomic within one transaction, not legally final as SDX or Fnality are.
  • "Official register" – the legal register is the issuer's or the TA's.
  • "Compliant with Reg S / Reg D", "ISO 20022-compliant", "travel-rule compliant" – until the adapters and partners exist.
  • "Immutable ledger" – selectors are immutable, storage is not (G7); say "no key changes a balance outside issue and redeem, under the upgrade delay".
  • "ERC-7943 conformant" while forcedTransfer is unimplemented; "bank-grade MPC" for a multisig; "insured"; "multi-chain"; "audit-ready" before SOC 1 / SOC 2 Type II; "agents cannot exceed mandates" before consume is called.
  • Allowed and honest: "auditable by construction – every balance, figure and term reconstructible from public events and signed records without Stobox; assurance (SOC 1, SOC 2 Type II) in progress, target period [date]."

7 · Institutional-grade criteria

Added to the four levels on the hub

Custodian says "we want this" when

  1. G1–G3 closed and the internal audit of the frozen scope published; an external audit when a client asks.
  2. The auditor pack rebuilds from eth_getLogs in their hands, G12 closed.
  3. Custodian omnibus is a first-class holder with attributed beneficiaries.
  4. ISO 20022 seev export exists; seev.033 instructions import.
  5. DTI, ISIN and LEI readable on the instrument.
  6. Upgrade delay on everything, a public change calendar.
  7. Instructing-party mandates enforced by contracts.
  8. DR document, RTO/RPO, runbooks, SLA classes.
  9. A second settlement asset beside USDC.
  10. The SCO60 one-pager exists and names Group 2; a permissioned deployment option is documented.

Auditor relies when

  • Controls exist per assertion – existence, completeness, rights, valuation, cut-off – each with an evidence artefact, owner and frequency.
  • The pack carries manifest, ABI and event schema, a reconstruction script the auditor runs, the register at a block with its hash, the three-way reconciliation, oracle history with signer keys and evidence, Terms root and JSON, key-management evidence, the change log and a cut-off sheet.
  • SOC 1 covers transaction processing for the client's statements; SOC 2 Type II shows the controls operated for a period; carve-outs name Stripe, KMS, the Base sequencer and Eqvista.
  • A key file maps subject ids to persons under NDA, or existence by person cannot be tested.

Compliance officer approves when

  • An AML programme, an MLRO, sanctions cadence, incident and breach reporting, complaints, conflicts (Eqvista, related-party issuers), blackout rules, marketing approval and geo-gating, record retention and DORA addenda exist in writing.
  • Every compliance decision leaves a trail: RuleFailed and setter events on chain, a signed off-chain decision log with a nightly anchor.
  • The Board's perimeter is settled by counsel; the related-party sale of STBX has holder terms, an amended PPM and tranches.
  • Surveillance runs: self-trade by subject, layering, insider windows, oracle front-running.

Buyer signs when

  • The RFP scores ≥ 40 of 72 after Phase 3 and > 50 after Phase 4 (the 72 questions live in _internal/RFP-72.md), and the "never" rows are canon, not omissions.
  • One signed custodian partner and one signed TA or administrator exist.
  • The enterprise package carries SLA 99.9% with credits, an insurance limit, verified escrow of the closed services, audit rights, a sandbox tenant, and one annual line with instrument-count tiers – never AUM basis points.
  • The price list no longer contradicts the canon.
