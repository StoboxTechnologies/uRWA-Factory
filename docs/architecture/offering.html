STV4 Offering

The regulatory route of an offering is chosen above the Factory – which entity issues, under which exemption, through which licensed intermediary – and the Factory executes the chosen regime as an immutable contract. The gap that matters: today the regime field sets nothing, and five defects in OfferingRegistry let a per-investor limit be split, a rule be removed mid-offering and an offering be settled early by anyone.

1 · The layers

From issuer to reporting, and the document on every arrow

Eight layers, top to bottom. Each arrow is bound by a document; three exist in the April 2026 pack, the rest are named. The two reds are the holder-facing terms (absent) and the regime switch in the Factory (no-op). Where Stobox sits: technology provider throughout, never the BD, never the venue.

Defects in today's code (verified 25.09.2026)

  1. Per-investor limits count one transaction, not the total. _bounds checks only the current purchase (:182); Purchase.subject is written as bytes32(0) (:211); paidBySubject is declared and unused (:74). Splitting a purchase bypasses maxPerInvestor. Blocks Reg CF, ECSP and EU thresholds.
  2. removeRule works in any state (:481): an operator can remove the accreditation rule mid-Reg D. After activate, only additions may be allowed.
  3. settle from Active, by anyone (:341): an offering can be closed early the moment the soft cap is met. Reg CF requires 21 days minimum and notice of early close.
  4. forceStatus moves to any state, including out of Settled (:520). Needs an explicit transition list.
  5. PurchaseRecorded fires on purchase and on delivery (:228, :401): event-based reporting doubles the sales in Form D and EU notifications.

Missing elements

  • Per-country counters (investors and amount, rolling 12 months, per offering and per issuer) kept in the registry and passed through RuleContext, so rules stay pure.
  • Art 1(4)(b) counts offerees, not buyers – uncountable on chain; hence lots and prices visible only after KYC, and an issuer claim offering.marketing.controls* with the hash of the geo-gating policy.
  • trancheId, closedAt, dcpEnd = closedAt + DCP; delivery lock-up = max(lockupUntil, settledAt + dcp).
  • intermediary (BD or portal: CRD / LEI hash), escrowAgent, docsHash (PPM, subscription), formDRef / formCRef / prospectusRef; settle requires the intermediary's closing signature where the regime demands one.
  • Signed subscription: purchase() takes the investor's EIP-712 signature over docsHash and representations; Reg S cat. 3 certification and 506(c) representations stored on the Purchase.
  • Withdrawal right: withdrawPurchase while Active (Reg CF until T−48h; EU PR Art 23 after a supplement; ECSP four-day reflection).
  • Reason codes: ERC-1404 codes today are pipeline stages (src/facets/RolesFacet.sol:96); one code covers DCP, Rule 144 and a freeze. Add bytes32 basis to Lockup (append-only) and codes by legal basis: 903/904, 144(d), 905, Reg CF 501, 12(g), FPO.
  • MaxHolders does not separate accredited from non-accredited; 12(g) needs a 500 non-accredited counter.
  • AttestorRegistry bound to keys: a KYC vendor cannot issue us.regd.accredited.

The residual Board risk, stated plainly

A lot is executed by an escrow contract and the operator holds forceCancel. That is a system that not only displays offers but executes them. The US Rule 3b-16(b) bulletin-board exclusion assumes no execution; under MiFID II this is closer to an MTF if EU residents are among the buyers; operator control over escrow raises a custody question. With US persons excluded the Exchange Act exposure is low but not zero: code, KMS and relay sit with Technologies. For the EU and UK the exposure is material. Counsel decides; the design does not.

Public wording (canon-aligned)

  • Not "exchange / marketplace / trading": "Compass Board – a bulletin board where admitted holders list their STBX at their own price. The parties transact; Stobox holds no funds, matches no orders and charges no fee."
  • Not "securities register": "the token's balance record on chain. The legal register is kept by [issuer / TA]."
  • Not "compliant with Reg S / Reg D": "the token's transfer rules exclude US persons and keep a 12-month lock-up on tokens bought from the company; the offering basis is stated in the offering documents."
  • Not "liquidity": "limited transferability between admitted holders; there is no public market."
  • Allowed: "issuance rail", "compliance layer", "technology provider".
5 · STBX

The regime stack for the Base issuance and the Board

STBX is an economic participation in Class C of Stobox Technologies (WY) through STEL (Doc 3.6 §1.2), and STEL is wholly owned by a US company – almost certainly not a foreign private issuer, so the underlying is equity of a non-reporting US issuer: Reg S category 3, and Rule 905 keeps what is bought restricted after the DCP.

Three facts that change the answer

  1. If "the company" sells continuously in Board lots, under 902(f) the DCP starts only when the distribution completes – for the whole offering it may never start. Hence tranches.
  2. STEL holds only the parent's shares: an Investment Company Act question (3(a)(1)(C)) that stays closed while there are no US holders – counsel.
  3. If even one STBX reached a US person (the Reg D perks removed on 25.09.2026), Reg S cleanliness is broken. Verify that no perk was ever paid out.

Coexistence with Reg D via Silicon Prairie

Rule 152(b)(2): Reg S offers and sales are not integrated with other offerings; the SEC's 2013 506(c) release says general solicitation in a parallel 506(c) is not a directed selling effort for Reg S – counsel to confirm the position applies. The instruments differ formally: Class C of Technologies directly versus STBX from STEL. Three residual risks: the PPM price $1.75 against a public reference price $1.99 (PPM amendment disclosing the parallel Reg S, the Board and the reference price); the STEL number in Offering Memorandum v3 is wrong (L-80); a Board page reachable from the US must carry no call to buy.

Recommended stack

  • Token: RegS preset plus us.regs.non_us_person*, SanctionsScreen(30d), MaxHolders, a country allow-list instead of a deny-list only; UK admitted through uk.fpo.**; China and the FATF lists denied.
  • Migration of 107 holders is not a new offering – a change of record form (MigrationClaim); lock-ups carry their dates. Conditions: no new consideration, no change of rights – counsel on 3(a)(9) / absence of a "sale".
  • Company lot in tranches, never continuous. Each lot or period gets trancheId and closedAt; buyerLockup = 12 months from purchase, stricter than a DCP from closing; the purchaser certification is signed at buy.
  • EU: qualified investors only (Art 1(4)(a)) plus ≤150 per state with country counters; lots visible only after KYC; placement in the EU through a MiFID partner. UK: certified HNW and sophisticated only.
  • BVI: the regime of the Board and the company lot is for BVI counsel.
6 · Decisions

For Gene, and for outside counsel

Gene

  1. Company lot in tranches or continuous? Recommended: tranches.
  2. Admit the EU to the Board before a MiFID partner exists? Recommended: no – non-EU, non-US and UK under FPO first.
  3. Commission STBX Token Terms (STEL ↔ holders) as the condition of the Base issuance.
  4. Narrow TRA §2.1: STEL issues only its own STBX; client projects run by Technologies as technology provider; consent to sublicense for the Board under Innovations.
  5. Fix order in OfferingRegistry: treasury drain and open createOffering, then aggregate limits by subject, no removeRule after activation, no settle from Active, bounded forceStatus – before the first Compass Issue client.

Outside counsel

  1. Is STBX an "equity security of a domestic issuer" for Reg S and Rule 905; look-through to Technologies as co-issuer?
  2. Is a company lot at a floating reference price a continuous offering under 902(f); are tranches with closedAt plus a 12-month buyer lock-up sufficient?
  3. STEL's Investment Company Act status and the conditions under which it stays outside.
  4. Is the Arbitrum → Base migration a "sale" or "offer"?
  5. Board: does the 3b-16 bulletin-board exclusion survive escrow execution; MTF qualification in the EU and UK; the custody question raised by forceCancel.
  6. EU PR after the Listing Act: exact thresholds, application date (05.06.2026 in the brief) and national options per target state; UK POATRs exemptions and the small-offer threshold.
  7. Is a placement agent needed for the company lot in the EU and UK, or does "issuer sells itself" suffice?
  8. May the minimum investment from bounds serve as "reasonable steps" under the March 2025 SEC staff letter in the Silicon Prairie round?
