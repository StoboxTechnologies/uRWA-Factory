Dependency rules

  1. The token depends on IPolicySet and IIdentityRegistry as interfaces only. Any implementation

satisfying the interface works.

  1. The token does not depend on the passport, the factory or the offering registry. It functions with

all three absent.

  1. Rules depend on IIdentityRegistry and on token view functions. They never write.
  2. The passport depends on nothing. It is written to, never read from, by the token.
  3. No contract in the open distribution references a Stobox address, the STBU token, or any Stobox

contract. This is an enforced test invariant.
